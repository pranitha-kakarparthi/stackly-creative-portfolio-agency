Hyperion Audio: Pure Acoustic Precision.

An interactive WebGL 3D exploded hardware experience, acoustic driver simulation, and direct-to-consumer flagship digital storefront.

The Challenge

Making Invisible Acoustic Physics Visually Irresistible

Hyperion engineered revolutionary planar-magnetic drivers and binaural spatial transducers. Yet standard photography failed to convey the aerospace aluminum acoustic chamber and multi-layered magnetic diaphragm.

Audiophiles and studio sound engineers demanded technical transparency and proof of driver craftsmanship before committing to premium $1,800 monitor headphones.

Strategic Execution

Interactive 3D Hardware Deconstruction

We gave users full kinetic control to pull apart the headphone architecture layer-by-layer in real-time WebGL.

01. Pure Monochromatic Hierarchy

Deep velvet carbon blacks and warm stone whites create a quiet backdrop, allowing the luminance of each fixture to radiate without distraction.

02. Bespoke Typographic Tokens

High-contrast editorial serif headings combined with architectural neo-grotesque numerals reflecting blueprints and technical dimensions.

03. WebGL Photometric Shader

Clients can dynamically simulate kelvin color temperatures (2700K to 5000K) and lumen output against raw concrete and marble surfaces in real time.
