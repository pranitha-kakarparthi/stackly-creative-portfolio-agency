What You Receive in the Lab Handover

Every asset is meticulously engineered for immediate production deployment.

01. Master Mark Geometry

Mathematical vector construction, responsive micro-scale adaptations, dark/light variants, Favicons, and SVG optimization.

02. Chromatic Token Matrix

WCAG AAA and AA compliant color palettes (including Canyon, Buttercream, and Morning Sky tones) with dark mode variables.

03. Typographic Hierarchy

Curated font pairings, fluid clamp-based sizing scales, line height mathematics, and character tracking specifications.

04. Figma Variable Systems

Turnkey Figma component library with auto-layout v5, multi-theme switching, and developer inspect documentation.

05. Editorial Brand Voice Bible

Comprehensive guidelines for copywriting, headline tone, executive communications, error states, and social presence.

06. Physical & Merch Collateral

Print-ready packaging templates, business cards, branded stationery, and architectural environmental signage specs.

Lab Workflow

The 4-Week Brand Incubation Sprint

Structured, milestone-driven delivery ensuring total executive alignment.

Week 1

Market Ethnography

Stakeholder interviews, customer sentiment analysis, and competitor visual mapping.

Week 2

Archetype Directions

Presentation of 3 divergent aesthetic concepts rendered across real-world digital touchpoints.

Week 3

Systemic Refinement

Fleshing out the selected direction into typography tokens, color modes, and layout patterns.

Week 4

Production Handover

Delivery of Figma source files, exported SVG vectors, code tokens, and team onboarding session.
